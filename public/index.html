Welcome To Task Manager

Welcome to Task Manager, your all-in-one productivity hub designed to keep your daily goals organized and effortless. Built with a modern full-stack web interface, our platform lets you seamlessly create, track, and manage your to-do items in real time. Say goodbye to scattered lists and hello to a clutter-free, focused workflow that helps you get things done faster. Task Manager is a lightweight, intuitive web application designed to help individuals streamline their daily routines. Powered by Node.js, Express, and MongoDB, it offers real-time task updates, clean completion tracking, and quick deletion—giving you complete control over your productivity anytime, anywhere.
